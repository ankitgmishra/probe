# Clay Software Usage in Sales and Growth Teams

*Generated: 2026-05-15 01:26 UTC*

---

## Executive Summary

Based on the evidence, Clay software is primarily used by sales teams for prospecting and transforming traditional sales operations. While growth teams also use Clay, its platform aims to power data enrichment and GTM motions, suggesting a stronger connection to sales teams.

---

## Key Findings

1. Clay's primary use case is in sales prospecting (E4).
2. Growth teams use Clay to build outbound campaigns faster and power their data enrichment and GTM motions (E15, E19).
3. The majority of Clay's customers are SaaS platforms focused on solving specific operational problems (E21, E22).


**Strong evidence supports:**

- ✅ E3: Clay's gross margins look more like a data-heavy SaaS firm.
- ✅ E5: Clay’s platform aims to transform traditional sales and marketing operations.

**Limited evidence (requires further validation):**

- ⚠️ E24: Clay is the right tool for SaaS teams that have a sending stack, time to build, and someone on the team who likes data workflows.
